History of Tuy Senior High School

The Senior High School in Tuy was established in the town of Tuy in 2016. Out of the desire and support, Mayor Jose Jecerell C. Cerrado, the concurrent Mayor of Tuy donated a land area of 0.47 hectares for the school's first building in the year 2014. It was located beside the Municipal Health Center facing Our Lady of Peace Academy and Gregorio Paradero Elementary School. From the national road, the air distance of the school is approximately 500 meters.

The primary aim is in consonance of the national objective of protecting and promoting the right of every Filipino to quality, equitable, culture-based, and complete basic education.

The construction of the building started in the year 2015 through the continuous support of Dr. Gregorio V. Meneses, Public Schools District Supervisor. Five classrooms of Gregorio Paradero Elementary School were ceded by Mr. Francisco E. Matalog, Principal II of the same school to Tuy Senior High School administration as temporary house while waiting for the turnover of the newly constructed building at Luna St. Tuy, Batangas. The management of the school was placed in the hand of Mr. Godofredo P. Marasigan - Principal II of Jose Lopez Manzano Tuy National High School as focal person.

The school was composed of one principal II, seven full-time teaching staff namely Mrs. Elsie M. De Los Reyes - Master Teacher II, Mrs. Jesusa Ravela - Teacher III, Mr. Restituto M. Mendoza - Teacher III, Mr. Joel L. Macalalad - Teacher II, Mrs. Maria Shiela D. Bayungan - Teacher I, Mr. Angelito B. Panganiban - Teacher II, Ms. Rosetti Ednaco - Teacher II and Mr. Teotimo R. Romarante - Teacher II - part-timer. For the non-teaching personnel: Mrs. Alice B. Umandap - Administrative Officer II, Mr. Dindo M. Diaz - School Registrar, one utility personnel and two security guards. While the first to become PTA(Parent-Teachers Association) was Mr. Jacinto P. Gemparo and Noelson A. Codizal, the first SSG(Supreme Student Government) president.

For the meantime Mrs. Jesusa Ravela became the Officer-in-Charge of Tuy Senior High School from August 17, 2016 up to the time that Mr. Francisco E Matalog, Principal II took over on February 18, 2017 It was also then that the school was granted the occupancy permit to its new building as the new school premises.

Mr. Matalog continued the work of infusing dynamism and tremendous changes and developments in all area and levels of senior high school operation. During this time, he was carrying out such plans through a process-oriented, principle-based participatory management approach to organizational development.

In spite of being a neophyte, the school managed to offer Humanities and Social Studies (HUMSS), Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics (STEM), Accountancy and Business Management (ABM), Industrial Arts - Electrical Installations and Maintenance (EIM), Information and Communication Technology (ICT) and Food and Beverages (F and B) for Technical and Livelihood Education.

Meanwhile, as the population of students grew, in the school year 2017-2018 with additional 460 Grade 11 students enrolled, the local government and stakeholders saw the urgency of constructing what would be the school's second building. More importantly, the employment of new 18 teachers and ancillaries was added.

The following school year 2018-2019, the total number of teaching and non-teaching personnel rises to 36, This number is to cater to 891 (First Semester) and 852 (Second Semester) learners enrolled in Grade 11 and Grade 12 under Academic Track - HUMSS, ABM, STEM, and TVL Track - EIM, HE, ICT, and an additional of new strand, Automotive. Also, a new building was constructed during this school year to cater the increasing number of enrollees.

In school year 2019-2020, it is reported to have an enrolment count of 1,037 learners (First Semester) and 994 (Second Semester) for Grade 11 and Grade 12 under the same tracks and strands offered the previous year. This was also the year where the school faced circumstances brought about by the Taal Volcano eruption and COVID-19 lockdown that forced the school to offer distance learning.

It was in the next school year, 2020-2021, that the school embraced pure modular distance learning due to COVID-19 lockdown. It is where the teaching and non-teaching staffdelivered printed modules to different barangays within and outside Tuy district with the help of stakeholders. With this, the school offered a new strand under the TVL track which is Agri-Fishery. Moreover, there are 37 teaching and non-teaching staff and the school noted 1,243 (First Semester) and 1,214 (Second Semester) enrollees in this school year. Also, this year is highlighted by the supervision and governance of the school head, Mrs. Ma. Verla Africa Alvaran.

In the school year 2021-2022, the school offered a blended learning modality - combination of modular and face-to-face learning - which was offered at the latter part of the school year. The student population increases to 1,387 (First Semester) and 1,331 (Second Semester). Also, the number of teachers increased to 42. The partnership of the school and external stakeholders became stronger this year during the delivery of learning materials to the barangays.

The following school year 2022-2023, Tuy Senior High School, opened the school for full in-person classes. With this, the Agri-Fishery strand is not offered this school year. 1,370 (First Semester) and 1,334 (Second Semester) learners attend face-to-face classes guided by 41 teaching and non-teachig personnel. With an increase in student population, the Evardome Building is occupied by STEM learners. It is also this school year that Tuy Senior High School welcomes new Teacher-in-Charge, Sir Ronel A. Gomez, Head Teacher I - effectivity date February 6, 2023.
