Facilities


Administrative Offices

The Administrative Office sets a day-to-day activities that are related to the maintenance of an office building, financial planning, record keeping and billing, personal development, physical distribution and logistics, within an organization.



Office of The Head Teacher

The Office of the Head Teacher/Teacher-in-Charge is in charge of the over-all administrative and academic concerns of the school.

Office of The Registrar

The Office of the Registrar is responsible for a variety of services for its constituents. These include student admission and registration; grade reporting, transcript of records generation; and the maintenance of academic records.

Faculty

The teachers' room – also known as the faculty lounge – is one of the most important rooms for educators. The teachers' room is the place where teachers spend time on teaching, research, student advising, clinical activities and various kinds of professional service on committees and in administrative or advisory roles.



Laboratories



Stem Laboratories

Science Labs provide students with various opportunities to learn and experiment, which plays a crucial role in the ongoing intellectual development of students at any academic level. Science labs give students the time, space, and resources to explore and experiment.

Computer Laboratory

Computer labs provide students with hands-on learning opportunities, allowing them to apply theoretical concepts in a practical setting. They can experiment with software applications, explore the internet, and learn programming languages, among other things.

Baking Laboratory

Baking Laboratory is designed for demonstrating the baking characteristics of the various wheat classes through instructional courses. The addition of specialty ingredients can be demonstrated in pan breads, hearth breads, buns, cakes, cookies and other baked goods. The Laboratory is also available for product development, product testing and specialized training.

Electrical Laboratory

Electrical lab a specialist lab where students are able to practically learn and improve their skills in key areas, such as wiring, lighting, and many more

Automotive Laboratory

The Automotive Lab functions as a student training center, which performs vehicle repairs related to specific course content.
