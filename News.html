Tuy Senior High School Journalists conquers Congressional District 1 School Press Conference Despite Short Preparation

Tuy District representatives from Tuy Senior High School managed to secure their places in the Con- gressional District 1 School Press Conference despite criticisms leveled against the short preparation period given to the participants.

Some argued that this gave the Tuy journalists an unfair disadvantage over their competitors, who may have had more time to prepare for the Congressional District 1 event held at Gregorio Paradero Central Elementary School on May 13.

"Syempre grabe yung pressure, kasi feeling ko mas napaghandaan nila yung laban. One day preparation lang naga wa namin e, siguro yung iba more than 2 or 3 days pa nag reready. Feeling ko trained na trained 'yung iba based sa mga awards na nakuha nila. Talagang hakot award yung iba." a journalist stated.

In spite of the matter, Tuy Senior High School journalists were able to secure all places and defended themselves, saying that they worked hard and happy for doing their best without expecting to win.

"Hindi ko akalain na makakapasok ako don. I mean, super gagaling ng mga kalaban ko. Right from the start of the competition nakita ko kung gaano sila kapulido gumawa. To be honest, medyo nawalan na 'ko ng pag asa kapag nakikita ko yung gawa nila. Kaya ganon na lang yung tuwa noong nalaman kong may place ako." a journalist added.

Monthly Bill Surges to 100K, Electric Meter Malfunctions Come Under Suspicion

Authorities of Tuy Senior High School suspected the potential of electric meter malfunctions as electric bill inflated to PHP 105 016.21 during month of February to March. In the statement of Ma'am Jollie Ann S. Avejero, Admin Officer II, she implied the abnormalities in the electric meter as a possible cause of sudden hike of electric bill, which according to them, was speeding at an abnormal rate even during the school break on holy week.

"We for Gender Equality and Inclusive Society"
TSHS Celebrates National Women's Month

Tuy Senior High School celebrated the National Women's Month this March together with its stakeholders. This year's theme, which is "WE for Gender Equality and Inclusive Society" honors the achievements and contribution of women in the society and aims to redefine women as well as share information regarding netiquette and cyberbullying.
