THE DEPED MISSION

To protect and to promote the right
of every Filipino to quality,
equitable culture-based and
complete basic education where:

Students learn in a child-
friendly, gender-sensitive, safe and
motivation environment.

Teachers facilitate learning and
constantly nurture every learner.

Administrators and staff, as
stewards of the institution, ensure
an enabling and supportive
environment for effective learning
to happen.

Family community, and other
stakehodersa re actively engaged
and share responsibility for
developing life long learners.
