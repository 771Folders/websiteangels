Announcement!

Attention students, parents, and faculty member!We have some exciting news and important dates to share with you. Please take note of the following announcement:

Early Registration: We are pleased to announce that early registration for upcoming academic year will take place from May 9 to June 10, 2023. This is an oppurtunity for both returning and new students to secure their spots and ensure a smooth transition for the next school year. Don't miss out this chance to secure your place at TUY Senior HighSchool.

Join us for Showtime! We have an amazing program planned for all of you. Get ready for the day filled with fantastic performances including Singing, captivating intermission numbers, amd exciting booths. Additionally, for all the sports enthusiasts out there, we will be hosting a volleyball and basketball from 1pm to 4pm. Come and support your classmates as they showcase their skills on court. Save the date, and be prepared for an unforgettable experience.

End of School Year 2022-2023: The end of the current school year is quickly approaching. We would like to remind everyone that the last day of classes for the 2022-2023 academic year will be on July 7, 2023. It's been a year filled with growth, accomplishments, and memories, and we are immensely proud of all our students' hard work.

Back to School Year 2023-2024: We hope you have enjoyed a restful and rejuvenating summer break because it's time to gear up for the new academic year! Mark your calendars as we welcome you back to school on August 28, 2023, for the start of the 2023-2024 academic year. We look forward to embarking on another exciting journey of learning and personal development together.

Please make sure to stay updated with our official communication channels for any further announcements or changes to these dates. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate yo reach out to our administration office.

Thank you, and we can't wait to see you all soon!
